⚔️ Job Change Guide

📅 Guide

This guide was extracted from the Echoes of Morroc Discord server.
Each entry lists the key NPCs with map coordinates, requirements, clear step-by-step directions, and the payoff for finishing the quest.

Merchant

Starting NPC: Merchant Representative Ellen — Orphanage (va_orpha 196,300)

Requirements: Orphan job (no extra prerequisites).

Steps:

  • Speak with Ellen about taking up the merchant life.
  • Confirm you accept the restrictions for low-level merchants.
  • Receive your license and new title immediately.

Reward: Job change to Merchant and a Merchant License.

Thief

Starting NPC: Lydia the Thief — Orphanage (va_orpha 64,41)

Requirements: Orphan Job Level 10 and 500 zeny.

Steps:

  • Ask Lydia for the Thief test and show your readiness.
  • Return with 500 zeny to be welcomed into the guild.

Reward: Job change to Thief along with a starter dagger.

Vagabond

Starting NPC: Jobless Orphan — Orphanage (va_orpha 17,18)

Requirements: Orphan Base Level 15 or higher with zero zeny on hand.

Steps:

  • Tell the Jobless Orphan you want to leave the path of regular jobs.
  • Return after reaching Base Level 15 to embrace exile.
  • Accept the offer to "walk free" and abandon structured classes.

Reward: Job change to Vagabond.

Raider

Starting NPC: Wandering Knight — Payon Field 9 (pay_fild09 334,264)

Requirements: Orphan Job Level 10.

Steps:

  • Ask the knight about fighting with courage.
  • Train to Job Level 10 if needed and show you are ready.
  • Accept the spear and the Raider title.

Reward: Job change to Raider and a novice spear.

Trickster

Starting NPC: Lorelei Sorin — Orphanage (va_orpha 376,129)

Requirements: Orphan Job Level 10.

Steps:

  • Chat with Lorelei and choose the cold-hearted reply to unlock her trust.
  • Touch the wisp in the same room (va_orpha 391,133) to reach the hidden chamber.
  • Speak with Serin inside the crypt (que_trck01 197,198) and pass her playful number-guessing challenge.

Reward: Job change to Trickster plus Serin's scythe.

Jester

Starting NPC: Pierrot — Prontera (prontera 105,64)

Requirements: Orphan Job Level 10.

Steps:

  • Meet Pierrot in Prontera and accept his invitation.
  • Find his "apprentice" south of the city (prt_fild08 55,57).
  • Survive the "Joking Around" obstacle course and pick your preferred reward card.

Reward: Job change to Jester plus a Joker's Wild card and the suit you selected.

Judge

Starting NPC: Leon Damon — Orphanage (va_orpha 310,326)

Requirements: Orphan Base Level 15 and Job Level 10.

Steps:

  • Speak with Leon in the Orphanage, then meet him inside Prontera Castle (prt_cas 94,254).
  • Petition King Nihil (prt_cas 94,297) to begin the trials.
  • Earn approval from every royal family:
    • Spica Nerius — Finance Wing (prt_cas 88,292): show at least 1,000 zeny or donate 10,000.
    • Helmut Lugenburg — Diplomacy Wing (prt_cas 79,271) and his envoy in Yuno (yuno 162,315).
    • Poe Richard — Military Wing (prt_cas 108,281): bring a Knife with random options from Porings.
    • Isenhardt Walter — Exploration Wing (prt_cas 80,275): automatic approval.
    • Ernst Gaebolg — Strategy Wing (prt_cas 79,279): pass his rotating five-question quiz.
    • Katrin Wigner — History Wing (prt_cas 112,277): clear her ten-question lore exam.
    • Peter Heine — Social Wing (prt_cas 108,276) and the grieving girl in Prontera (prontera 275,340).
  • Report back to King Nihil to receive the royal seal.
  • Meet Catherine Gaebolg in the castle garden (prt_cas 105,80) for the formal induction.

Reward: Job change to Judge plus the Judge badge, a revolver, and basic ammunition.

Dark Knight

Starting NPC: Strange Glass — Prontera Inn (prt_in 78,87)

Requirements: Orphan Base Level 15 or higher and Job Level 10.

Steps:

  • Touch the cursed mirror in the inn to face your inner shadows.
  • Endure thirteen waves of specters without giving in to their whispers.
  • Accept the offered pact to wield vengeance as a Dark Knight.

Reward: Job change to Dark Knight.

Illusionist

Starting NPC: Broken Clock — Orphanage (va_orpha 272,379)

Requirements: Orphan Base Level 15, Job Level 10, and 1,000 zeny.

Steps:

  • Repair the clock by adjusting the second gear, the small needle, and finally the large needle.
  • Enter the hidden portal and meet the Familiar Girl in Lutie (xmas_in 111,107).
  • Purchase the Kimi doll from the machine (xmas_in 169,22) and speak with her again.
  • Embrace your memories to bind Kimi as your avatar.

Reward: Job change to Illusionist and the Illusionist glove.

Mimic

Starting NPC: Mysterious Mirror — Orphanage (va_orpha 390,193)

Requirements: Orphan Base Level 25 or higher.

Steps:

  • Approach the mirror after meeting the level requirement.
  • Choose to look at your reflection and accept the strange book it offers.

Reward: Job change to Mimic and a Mimic grimoire.

Ronin

Starting NPC: Old Master Dio — Amatsu (amatsu 282,140)

Requirements: Orphan Job Level 10.

Steps:

  • Agree to undergo Master Dio's three patience trials.
  • Listen carefully to the Dreaming Lady's travel story (amatsu 220,211) and answer her follow-up question.
  • Meditate beside the Clear Lake for one uninterrupted minute (amatsu 278,252).
  • Watch the Windy Spot (amatsu 265,190) and dodge the gust when it arrives.
  • Report back to Master Dio for your void-forged saya.

Reward: Job change to Ronin and the Void Saya.

Peacekeeper

Starting NPC: Peacekeeper Valen — Frontier Outskirts (front_out 245,210)

Requirements: Orphan Base Level 15 and Job Level 10.

Steps:

  • Offer to help Valen recover the stolen Peacekeeper trinket.
  • Collect the judge's torn cloth by the docks (front_out 142,112).
  • Question the workshop laborer (front_out 179,152) and the airship attendant (front_out 179,200).
  • Inspect the chipped stone on the airstrip wall (front_out 185,245) and retrieve the fallen trinket below (front_out 185,248).
  • Return the Dread Razor Trinket to Valen and accept induction.

Reward: Job change to Peacekeeper, the Oathguard whip, and the reclaimed trinket.

Assassin & Rogue

How to Enter: Take the hidden hatch in Sograt Desert (moc_fild16 205,294) down into the Assassin Guild.

Starting NPC: Assassin Guild Receptionist — Assassin Guild Lobby (assas_03 80,16)

Requirements: Thief Job Level 50.

Steps:

  • Talk to the Guild Captain and accept the test.
  • Complete the shared guild evaluations: the stealth course, hidden passages, and combat trials.
  • Report to the guild master to choose between the Assassin's blade path or the Rogue's subterfuge.

Reward: Job change to either Assassin or Rogue.

Unchained Thief

Starting NPC: Alkouskou — Unchained Training Hall (va_orpha 287,113)

Requirements: Thief Job Level 50, no prior specialization, and an MVP Prize Box with random options.

Steps:

  • Speak with Alkouskou about forging your own path.
  • Present the MVP Prize Box to prove your dedication.
  • Complete the brief meditation to unlock your hybrid potential.

Reward: Job change to Unchained Thief and immediate access to hybrid skill ranks.

Phantom Thief

Starting NPC: Melody — Unchained Training Hall (va_orpha 287,132)

Requirements: Unchained Thief at Base Level 99 and Job Level 50, 200 Distortion Shards.

Steps:

  • Return to Melody once you reach the peak of Unchained mastery.
  • Trade the 200 shards for her advanced instruction.
  • Practice her routine to absorb the Phantom Thief repertoire.

Reward: Job change to Phantom Thief.

Shadowseer

Starting NPC: Lost Spirit — Niflheim (niflheim 327,70)

Requirements: Thief Job Level 50.

Steps:

  • Approach the spirit and allow it to read the mark in your eyes.
  • Accept its guidance to balance light and shadow.
  • Embrace the new perspective it grants.

Reward: Job change to Shadowseer plus the Specter Dagger.

Prowler

Starting NPC: Edmond — Lighthalzen (lighthalzen 308,250)

Requirements: Thief Job Level 50.

Steps:

  • Agree to test Edmond's prototype armguard and accept the device.
  • Hunt Orphan Hunters near the Nomad Village until the armguard records enough data.
  • Return to Edmond before exceeding Base Level 80 to finalize the upgrade.

Reward: Job change to Prowler along with the enhanced armguard and Rope Kunai.

Legend

Starting NPC: Wandering Vagabond — Dicastes Outskirts (dicast_out 106,168)

Requirements: Vagabond Job Level 70.

Steps:

  • Hear the rumor from the wandering vagabond about strange power.
  • Visit the Mysterious Man studying the Heart of Power (snow_fild05 239,55).
  • Break the six seals within the Heart of Power and defeat Legendary Zack.

Reward: Job change to Legend.

Dracomancer

Starting NPC: Strange Person — Thanatos Tower Approach (tha_scene01 140,191)

Requirements: Raider Job Level 50.

Steps:

  • Accept the challenge to raise dragons under Nydhogg's guidance.
  • Solve the pillar trials and combat tests within the dragon sanctum.
  • Earn Tiamat's blessing to carry the dragons' orbs.

Reward: Job change to Dracomancer.

Revenant

Starting NPC: Recluse Trickster — Niflheim (niflheim 93,227)

Requirements: Trickster Job Level 70.

Steps:

  • Ask the recluse to arrange an audience with Lady Hel.
  • Follow Serin through the Long Night and subdue the rebellious Reapers (que_sign01 49,27).
  • Defeat Revenant Ebel (que_sign01 55,217) and survive Lady Hel's ritual (que_sign01 45,227).

Reward: Job change to Revenant, a Reaper egg, and a reforged scythe.
